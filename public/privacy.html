Privacy Policy

Vyapar Billing · Vyapara Bakery Supplies · Last updated 13 October 2026

Vyapar Billing is the private billing and accounts system of Vyapara Bakery Supplies. It is used only by the shop's owner and staff; it is not offered to the public. This page explains what the app does with data, including data from Google accounts.

Data the app keeps

The app records the shop's own business records: bills, items and stock, purchases, suppliers, and customers' business details (bakery name, contact name, phone number, address, GSTIN and outstanding balance). These are stored on the shop's own computer and are used only to run the shop's billing, accounts and GST records.

Google account data

The shop owner can connect a Google account so the app can back up its records to Google Drive. When connected, the app:

  • uses the Google Drive "drive.file" permission to create a folder named "Vyapara Billing Backups", upload backup files into it, and delete old backup files it created. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Google Drive;
  • reads the account's email address, only to show which account the backups go to;
  • keeps the sign-in token on the shop's computer so backups continue without signing in again.

Google data is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than storing the shop's own backups. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app (Settings → Google Drive Backup → Disconnect), which also cancels the sign-in token, or from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backup files already in Google Drive stay there until the account owner deletes them.
